3 PATHWAYS

DSE / IB / A-Level,
pick the one that fits your child.

HKDSE, IBDP and A-Level — three mainstream tracks, each with its own positioning. We match the right one to your family goals, child ability, English level and target destination.

D HKDSE · MAIN LOCAL TRACK

Hong Kong Diploma of Secondary Education

A public exam unified by the HKEAA, launched in 2012. The only Chinese-medium international exam globally — the mainstream track for HK-resident students.

  • 5 grade levels (5** is the highest)
  • 4 core subjects + 2+ electives
  • Category A subjects + B applied learning + C other languages
  • Recognised by HK UGC 8, 138 mainland and 300+ overseas universities
  • Choosing science / M1/M2 extended maths = wider university course options
IB IBDP · INTERNATIONAL

International Baccalaureate Diploma

A 2-year pre-university programme designed by the IBO. Emphasises interdisciplinary thinking and global perspectives — broadly accepted by top universities worldwide.

  • 6 subject groups (language, second language, individuals & societies, sciences, mathematics, arts)
  • Emphasises critical thinking, CAS activities and the Extended Essay
  • 30+ international schools in HK offer IB
  • Pathways: top overseas universities + some HK universities accept IB to DSE bridge
  • Best for students with strong English and self-directed learning habits
AL GCE A-LEVEL · BRITISH

Advanced Level Qualification

The General Certificate of Education Advanced Level — a long-established British pre-university qualification with flexible subject combinations of 3-4 subjects.

  • Students pick 3-4 subjects for in-depth study
  • Deep subject mastery with clear exam structure
  • Directly recognised by top universities in the UK, Australia, Canada and Singapore
  • Some HK international and private schools offer A-Level
  • Best for students with clear goals and standout subject strengths
