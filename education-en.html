Education FAQ

您可能會問嘅事。

My grades aren't great—is associate the only path?
Not necessarily. We assess your GPA, IELTS, activities, family resources—3 viable paths. Some cases go direct, some via IB retake, some via 2+2. Key is objective assessment, not hard-selling one path.
How many revisions for PS and recommendations?
Unlimited. We aim for "you'd want to submit it yourself". Typical: PS 5-8 rounds, recommendations 2-3 rounds.
Do you keep supporting after enrollment?
Yes. 4 years post-enrollment: monthly parent reports, emergency support, summer internships, master's planning—all included.
