高 TTPS · High-income / top education

Top Talent Pass Scheme

Launched 2022. Category A (HK$2.5M+ annual income), B (top-100 global university + 3yr work experience in last 5), C (top-100 grad, <5yr experience). Fast approval — as quick as 4 weeks.

優 QMAS · Top talent

Quality Migrant Admission Scheme

New points system from Nov 2024 — meet 6 of 12 criteria covering age, education, language, work experience, income, business ownership. Achievement-based track also available for distinguished applicants.

專 ASMTP · Industry specialists

Admission Scheme for Mainland Talents

Two paths: entrepreneurs setting up a HK business (self-employed), or specialists hired by a HK company. Diploma+ qualifications + proven professional skills. Fast approval — ideal for those with a clear employer or business plan.

投 NEW CIES · High-net-worth

Capital Investor Entrant Scheme

Re-launched March 2024. For high-net-worth individuals — net assets of HK$30M+ held for 6 months prior, invest in permitted financial assets / non-residential property. 2 + 3 + 3 year visa, 7 years to PR.

學 IANG · Study → HK

Study Pathway & Associate Degree

Study at HK university / associate / postgrad — IANG visa to work in HK for 2 years after graduation, 7-year employment path to PR. For young professionals + family education dual-track.
