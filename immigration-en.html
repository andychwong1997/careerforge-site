4 Mainstream Pathways

Tailor your route to Hong Kong
around your background.

A

Top Talent Pass Scheme (Categories A / B / C)

High-income professionals (Category A), graduates of the world's top 100 universities (Category B), or 5 years' work experience including 2 post-qualification (Category C). Dependants may apply in parallel. Processing time follows ImmD's published schedule.

ImmD Processing Timing subject to individual case and prevailing policy
Q

Quality Migrant Admission Scheme (QMAS)

General Points Test or Achievement-based Scheme, assessed across age, qualifications, work, language and family background. Renewal pattern is 3+3+2; 7 years of continuous ordinary residence qualifies you to apply for PR.

Points-based Assessment starts at 80 points · not auto-approved
E

Employment Visa (Top Talent / General)

Sponsored by a Hong Kong-registered company, the role must be one not readily filled by the local workforce. Suits candidates with a confirmed HK employer offer or those on intra-company transfer.

Sponsor Required Renewal depends on continuity of employment
I

Study & Associate Degree Pathways

Secure a student visa through a Hong Kong bachelor's, master's or associate degree programme, then apply for the IANG non-local graduate visa after graduation. A common starting point for the 7-year PR pathway.

D

Dependant Visa

Once the principal applicant is approved, the spouse and unmarried children under 18 may apply in parallel or subsequently, enabling them to live and study in Hong Kong.

R

Renewal & 7-Year PR Planning

For existing visa holders: renewal document preparation, ordinary-residence record review, avoiding day-counting pitfalls, and aligning the rhythm of your PR application with compliance requirements.

Latest ImmD Review Indicators

Renewal is no longer just a paperwork formality.
Are you covered on ImmD's four review dimensions?

The four dimensions below reflect the ImmD's heightened scrutiny of Top Talent Pass / QMAS / Employment visa renewals in 2024–2026, for reference only. Final decisions rest with the policies and outcomes in force at the time of application.

MPF

MPF — Proof of Genuine Local Employment

The ImmD will check MPF contribution records: contribution months, contribution amounts (consistent with declared salary), and whether the employer's name matches your application. Gaps or anomalous contributions will trigger review.

Mandatory Continuous contribution records are core renewal evidence
  • ✓ Contribution records fully aligned with employment period covered by visa
  • ✓ Contribution amounts consistent with the employment contract salary
  • ✓ Employer MPF account matches company registration details
TAX

Tax — Salaries Tax + Profits Tax

Renewal will check personal salaries tax and company profits tax / business registration. Anomalous tax filings (such as prolonged zero returns) are treated as evidence of "no real business".

CTR

Employment Contract — At Market Levels

The contract's position, compensation and scope of duties must align with industry market levels. The ImmD benchmarks against JobsDB / Labour Department median pay; unusually high or low pay will trigger review.

OPS

Company Operating Evidence Chain

Office lease, actual business contracts, bank statements, audited reports — all evidence that the company is genuinely operating, not a shell.

  • ✓ Physical office lease + utility bills
  • ✓ Service / sales contracts with third-party clients
  • ✓ Bank statements showing routine receipts and payments
RES

Ordinary Residence Records

Core to the 7-year PR pathway. The ImmD assesses patterns of habitual residence: days outside HK, family members in HK, assets in HK, social ties in HK. Absences over 6 consecutive months require reasonable explanation.

FIT

Industry / Role Alignment

Your renewal role must align with the "contributing to Hong Kong" sector declared at original application. Qualifications, professional credentials and experience should form a logical chain.

Visa FAQs

Questions you may have.

The following is general information, not legal advice. Individual cases follow the policies, requirements and decisions published by the ImmD and other relevant authorities at the time.

How should I choose between Top Talent Pass, QMAS and Employment Visa?
The three pathways differ in eligibility thresholds, renewal patterns and downstream obligations. Our approach: a 30-minute complimentary assessment based on your qualifications, work experience, family situation and goals, recommending the 1–2 most suitable routes with their trade-offs and risks for your consideration, so the final decision rests with you.
I've had a visa refusal — is there still hope?
After a refusal we can reassess, strengthen the documentation or consider an alternative route, but success still depends on the ImmD's prevailing standards and your current circumstances. We will run a 30-minute complimentary assessment first, then recommend a direction based on reality; we make no approval guarantees.
How long does a dependant visa take?
Dependants are typically processed alongside the principal applicant or queued by the ImmD after the principal approval; exact timing rests with the ImmD. We submit the dependant application promptly after the principal is approved, minimising document turnaround time.
Can the 7-year PR truly be achieved?
A PR application must meet the ImmD's ordinary-residence requirement, remain continuous throughout the visa period, and be filed per the then-current ImmD guidelines. We provide 7-year renewal and PR planning services: organising ordinary-residence records, avoiding day-counting pitfalls, and pre-reviewing each renewal file. Whether PR is ultimately granted is decided by the ImmD.
Does CareerForge offer any "approval guarantee"?
No, and we cannot guarantee renewals, PR, or any fixed timeline for approvals. All visa decisions are made independently by the Hong Kong Immigration Department. Our role is to provide document preparation, process follow-up and compliance guidance based on policy and case realities — we cannot make the decision on the ImmD's behalf.
Does the service cover children's education?
Yes. Aligned with your visa landing timeline, we help plan placements at local, international or boarding schools, and flag the key timing milestones for school applications. Education advice is anchored in family preferences, the child's aptitude and each school's admissions criteria in the relevant year.
